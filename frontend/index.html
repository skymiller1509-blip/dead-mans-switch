Dead Man's Switch

If you go quiet for N days, your funds go to your heir — automatically, on-chain.

A Reactive contract on Lasna testnet watches your vault on Base Sepolia. Every tick it checks how long you have been silent. If the silence reaches N days, it tells the vault to send everything to your heir. The vault checks the dates itself before moving anything, so a mistaken trigger can never pay out early.

Not connected.

2. Set up your switch

Fill these in, then press "Create vault". This deploys your vault on Base Sepolia (chain 84532). The Reactive contract on Lasna is already deployed by the project and pointed at your vault afterwards.

Keep a little extra ETH in the vault: the callback that releases the funds is billed to the vault in ETH, so the vault needs a small reserve on top of the amount you want your heir to receive.

Press this whenever you want to reset the clock. Depositing also counts.
